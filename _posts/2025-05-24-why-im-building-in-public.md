After 11+ years building systems at Amazon — supply chain optimization, seller platforms, identity verification — I'm starting something new alongside my day job: building developer tools powered by AI, and sharing the journey openly.

## Why Now

The generative AI wave isn't just changing what we build — it's changing *how* we build. I've spent years designing large-scale distributed systems. Now I want to apply that systems thinking to the tools developers use every day.

## What "Building in Public" Means to Me

It's not about broadcasting every commit. It's about:

- **Thinking out loud** — writing about architectural decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned
- **Sharing the messy middle** — not just polished launches, but the exploration phase
- **Creating accountability** — public commitment to ship, learn, and iterate

## What to Expect Here

This site is a home for:

1. **Technical deep-dives** on AI systems and software architecture
2. **Project updates** as I build developer tools
3. **Essays** on technology trends, systems thinking, and the craft of engineering

## The Bet

I believe the best developer tools of the next decade will be built by people who understand both large-scale systems *and* AI capabilities deeply. That intersection is where I want to play.

Let's see where this goes.
