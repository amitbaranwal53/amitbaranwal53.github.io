## Who I Am

I'm Amit Baranwal, a Senior Software Development Engineer at Amazon based in Bengaluru, India. Over 11+ years at Amazon, I've built systems across Fulfilment by Amazon, Supply Chain Optimisation, Identity Verification, and Seller Onboarding — working at the intersection of large-scale distributed systems and real-world logistics.

Before Amazon, I worked on Oracle's Recovery Manager (RMAN) and Zero Data Loss Recovery Appliance, building database restore and near-zero data loss features in C and Perl.
