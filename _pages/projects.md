An intelligent tool for navigating and understanding large codebases using generative AI. Ask questions about architecture, find patterns, and get contextual explanations without reading every file.

**Status:** In progress
**Stack:** Python, LLMs, Tree-sitter

## Living Docs Platform

Documentation that stays in sync with your code. Automatically detects drift between docs and implementation, suggests updates, and maintains a knowledge graph of your system's architecture.

**Status:** In progress
**Stack:** TypeScript, Knowledge Graphs, LLMs
